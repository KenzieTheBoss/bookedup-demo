Questions owners ask

Does it answer my phone?

No. Calls still ring you first. BookedUp only steps in when a call goes unanswered, by texting the caller back. It's text only.

Do I need new software?

No. It books into the Google Calendar you already use. If you run something else, tell me on the call.

Will it quote prices or promise things I can't do?

No. It doesn't quote prices. It collects the problem, the address and the urgency, and books a time from your real openings.

What about emergencies?

Gas smells, flooding, no heat in winter: it texts your phone immediately so you can decide what to do.

What if I don't like it?

You get a 30-day money-back guarantee. If it doesn't earn its keep in the first 30 days, you get your money back.
